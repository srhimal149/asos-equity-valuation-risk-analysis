# Credit Risk & Equity Valuation: ASOS Plc (LSE: ASC)

A quantitative equity analysis of ASOS Plc — a UK-listed online fashion retailer (LSE ticker ASC.L, Consumer Cyclical / Internet Retail) — built in a macro-enabled Excel workbook. The model integrates fundamental analysis, credit risk indicators, options-based risk management, real options valuation, and portfolio optimisation into a single end-to-end workflow.

## Overview

ASOS has faced significant operational headwinds in recent years — declining revenue, negative EBITDA, elevated leverage (debt-to-equity ~228%), and a beta above 2.0 — making it a compelling case study in distressed equity valuation and credit risk assessment for a consumer cyclical name.

This workbook brings together the techniques typically taught across separate finance modules (corporate valuation, risk management, derivatives, portfolio theory) and applies them to a single listed company.

## Contents

| Sheet | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Stock Financials | Headline metrics: market cap, P/E, EPS, margins, leverage, liquidity, beta, 52-week range |
| Income Statement | Five-year historical P&L with EBITDA, EBIT, normalised earnings |
| Balance Sheet | Five-year historical balance sheet, working capital, leverage breakdown |
| Cash Flow Statement | Operating, investing, and financing cash flows over five years |
| Price | Historical price series and market data |
| FM | Financial modelling and intrinsic valuation |
| Risk_Options | Options-based risk analysis and hedging |
| Real_Options | Real options valuation of strategic flexibility |
| Portfolio_Optimization | Mean-variance portfolio optimisation framework |
| Playground | Scratch space for sensitivity testing |

## Methods Applied

- **Equity valuation** — fundamental ratio analysis, multiples, intrinsic value
- - **Credit risk** — leverage, liquidity, and coverage indicators
  - - **Derivatives & hedging** — options-based risk metrics
    - - **Real options** — valuing strategic flexibility under uncertainty
      - - **Portfolio theory** — mean-variance optimisation and efficient frontiers
